巴夏：单相思的真正原因
原创 笑笑^_^学进 巴夏Bashar 2016-01-12



问：

What is true nature and purpose of unrequited love?

得不到的爱/暗恋/单相思 的真正的本质和的目究竟是什么？



巴夏：

Unrequited love?

得不到的爱？（unrequited意思：没有回报）



问：

Yes

是的



巴夏：

There's no such thing.

没有这种爱



If it is unrequited

如果是得不到的爱（单相思）



It is not love

那这不算爱



It is expectation.

而是期待



It's not allowing the relationship to be what it is.

是不允许当前的关系，是它所是的样子



It's not accepting the love in the form in which it actually comes in that relationship

说明不接受当下关系的发展现状



As being real enough,

认为关系不够真实



as being good enough.

爱得不够深（不够好）



That's the only thing that creates the idea of unrequited

这是导致 得不到的爱/单相思 的真正源头



Is you're not really paying attention to what it is

是你没把注意力放在当前关系状况上



You're only paying attention to what you expect it to be.

而只是把注意力放在自己所期待的上面



问：

This is the part about wanting your life to be a certain way

就是说，你想要的是“那种爱”



And not accepting the way in which you actually manifest something

而不接受你已然显化出来的“这种爱”



巴夏：

Yes

对了！



问：

And not looking for the way to use whatever is presented to you in a positive way,

不去想办法，积极地利用当前的关系



Rather than using it for in a sense of self diminishment

反而因为当前的现状，而自我消沉



巴夏：

Correct.

对的



问：

That's what gives the person who is experiencing the unrequited love

于是那些单相思中（得不到爱）的人



Feeling that sad feeling.

就会觉得很悲伤



巴夏：

Yes

没错！



Because the real unrequited love is that they're not loving themselves as much as they could.

真正得不到爱的人，是因为他们没给自己足够的爱



That's what's unrequited

于是就变成“得不到爱”



And they are simply getting a reflection of that

爱的人不爱他，只是他们不够爱自己的外在反射/投射



So the other persons is also reflecting to them that they don't accept themselves, and they don’t.

于是对方就不接受他，他便显化出“得不到爱”



The unrequited factor is within the person experiencing unrequited love, not on the other.

没人爱的真正原因，在自身，与他人无关（金钱也是）
