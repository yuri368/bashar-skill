问：

One last thing. The I-Ching, Can you tell me that?

最后一问题。你能跟我说说《易经》吗？



巴夏：

I-Ching is a remnant of an ancient technology

易经是一种古老技术的残留物



that understood certain resonant patterns,

该技术深谙某些谐振模式



And it is representative of some ancient devices

易经代表古代的某种设备



that was capable of replicating those energy patterns,

这种设备可以复制这些能量模式



for the purpose, again, of putting one in a proper state,

以便让人处于一种准确的状态



by which one could access more information, more knowledge,

在那种状态下，人们能够获得更多的信息与知识，



more connection to All That Is,

更容易与“一切所是”相连



and download, as you say, in your current language,

同时，用你们现在的话说，叫“下载”



whatever information or knowledge was required at that time.

下载在那个当下所需要的任何信息或知识
