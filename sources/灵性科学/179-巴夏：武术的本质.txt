It's all about harmonizing frequency.

Martial arts teaches you primarily, fundamentally, the idea of matching the resonant pattern of another

武术，本质上所教导的是练习与对手的谐振模式吻合



to such a degree that when you are engaged in that so-called martial art dance your matching their motion so precisely

在你们的对招时，你准确地匹配对方的动作（太极拳）



that there is a moment when neither of you are moving relative to one another

有那么一个瞬间，你与对手是相对静止的



and in that moment, they can be redirected in any direction whatsoever

在那个瞬间，你可以不费一丝一毫的力气，（四两拨千斤）



because it takes absolutely no effort to do so

轻松化解对方的招式，并将对手导向任何方向
