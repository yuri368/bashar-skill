我也总希望能得到他人的赞美与鼓励

But when I don't get that, I feel like I am alone and I feel like people forget about me.
如果没得到的话，我会感到孤单，会觉得人们把我忘记了

巴夏：
So you are not following your passion for its own sake.
这么说，你跟随激情，并不是因为激情本身

But for what you think it will get you
而是为了 你认为它能带给你的东西喽？

You have to follow your passion regardless of what people's response is
不管他人如何反应，你都应该跟随自己的激情

Because there's simply no other choice for you.
因为，你根本就没有其他选择

That's being who you really are.
这才是“做真正的自己”

It doesn't matter what other people think or what they give you.
别人怎么想，或者给了你什么，这并就不重要

You have to follow your passion for its own sake
你必须是因为激情本身，才跟随激情

Or you're not being pure about it.
否则，你的激情，就不纯粹

You're using it as a condition to get feedback, to be validated by the outside world.
你只是用它为自己创造条件，让自己获得外界的反馈与认可

The outside world cannot validate you
外界，无法认可你

You can only validate yourself.
你只能认可自己

So as soon as you start simply doing what you love for its own sake
所以，一旦你开始做你热爱的事情，仅仅是因为你热爱它

Not because of the feedback you're going to get
而不是因为你能收获多少“点赞”

Then it can expand.
那么，它（激情）就会扩散

And more people can find you who will support you
更多的人，会找到你、支持你

But you can't do it to get the support.
但你这么做，不能是为了获得支持

You have to support yourself.
你必须支持你自己

You have to know that you are valid.
你需要知道，你是有用的、有价值的
