问：
And a follow up question here is about the path of their desire
接下来的问题是：关于“自我渴望的道路”

And how their brain will just start planning and envisioning and getting expectations about this and that.
为什么我们的脑袋会开始想象与计划？为什么我们会期待发生这个，或那个？

And the idea of why does our consciousness do that?
为什么我们的意识会这样做？

And how do we manage that in relation to having zero expectation?
我们该如何管控自己，从而做到“零期待”地跟随我们的兴奋呢？

巴夏：
Because you don't quite yet understand and the physical mind thinks it's in control, which it's not
因为，你们还没有完全明白，而且，你的头脑认为它在掌控，其实不是

And doesn't quite yet understand that having a particular insistence on a particular outcome is actually a limitation.
你们也还没明白，对于某个特定结果的坚持，其实是一个限制

Because if you insist on a particular thing and something better could have happened, 
因为，如果你坚持某件事，而事实上，可能发生更好的事情

you have just prevented it from happening.
那么，你其实阻碍了它的发生

So learn the idea that insisting on a particular outcome may actually not be to your benefit
所以，你们要学会：坚持某个特定的结果，可能对你并没有好处

Because it actually prevents other maybe even better outcomes from happening.
因为，这会阻碍其他能带给你更好结果的可能性的发生
