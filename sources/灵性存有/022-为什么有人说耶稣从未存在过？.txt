There are many reasons why people may. Deny the existence of Yeshua, but Yeshua did exist.The idea, of course, is that doesn't mean that there weren't other stories from ascended masters incarnating on your planet that closely mirror the story of Yeshua as well.Because that has been repeated in your society throughout history to again build up your familiarity with how these things unfold and to put you in touch with the idea of your own self empowerment.So, yes. That story has been repeated incarnationally by many different entities.So the idea is that it was also repeated by Yeshua, and that is why it seems to be a mythological or legendary story.Nevertheless, Yeshua did exist.
