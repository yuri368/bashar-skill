问：你好，Tadaya。

Hello, Tadaya.

Narnia.

We have lots of wonderful questions from our viewers.

巴夏：好的。

All right. 

问：我将专注于人工智能的问题，因为这是我们今晚的大话题。

I'm going to focus in on AI, since that's our big topic tonight.

巴夏：好的。

All right. 

问：在这个人工智能时代，我对人类与自然界的关系的本质很好奇。你提到过我们是大自然的一部分，但灰人是如何失去与大自然的连接的？

In this AI age, I'm curious about the nature of human relationship with the natural world.You've mentioned that we're part of nature, but how did the Greys lose their connection to nature?

巴夏：他们选择成为灰人是因为他们已经失去了作为人类与自然的连接。灰人并没有失去连接。人类失去了连接，不得不变异成灰人才能生存。

They chose to become the Greys because they had lost their connection with nature as humans.The Greys didn't lose their connection. The humans lost their connection and had to mutate into the Greys to survive.

图片

所以这个想法是，他们作为人类变得过于关注技术，放弃了这个想法，忘记了他们与自然的连接。把他们的技术带得太远，破坏了他们的许多生态系统，从而通过破坏自然进一步剥夺了他们与大自然的连接。

So the idea is they became too focused on technology as humans, forsaking the idea, forgetting the idea of their connection with nature.took their technology too far, destroyed many of their ecosystems, thus depriving themselves further of their connection to nature by destroying it.

因此不得不变异自己才能在他们创造的环境中生存，而环境变得对他们来说越来越有毒。

And thus having to mutate themselves in order to survive in the environment they had thus created, which became more and more toxic to them.
