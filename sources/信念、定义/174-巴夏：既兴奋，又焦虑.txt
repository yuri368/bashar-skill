巴夏：既兴奋，又焦虑?
原创 巴夏Bashar 巴夏Bashar 2023-09-13 20:42



问：
When I get a big job, I get a lot of pleasure and anxiety
当我接了个“大单”，我会很开心，也会焦虑

Even though I want to people to know about me more, I get scared and ended up escaping from those situations.
虽然，我想让他人多了解我，但我也会害怕，最终，只能忍痛割爱，逃离现场。

巴夏：
This is the same thing though
其实，这是同一回事

You're giving over to the idea of outside validation.
你沉湎（屈服）于外界的认可

问：
But this pressure made me confuse if I want to do it or not.
但这种压力，让我很困惑，导致我不知道我是否想做这个工作

巴夏：
You are putting the pressure on yourself.
你自己给自己施加压力

You are overwhelming yourself, by the way, you're thinking about what it is you're doing.
你一直在想“我在干嘛？我在干嘛？”，这反而把你自己压得喘不过气来了

That's what we're attempting to tell you.
这正是我们想要告诉你的

You have to just do what you love to do for its own sake.
你只需要出于爱，而做自己所爱的事，就够了

It doesn't matter what other people think about it.
别人怎么想，并不重要

问：
But it sometimes make me excited, also worry at the same time.
有时候，它让我兴奋，但同时，也让我担忧

巴夏：
No, not at the same time
不，并不是同时

You go back and forth
你是来来回回

You cannot be excited and be in anxiety at the same time.
你无法同时既又兴奋，又焦虑

You are making a choice to leave your excitement and go into anxiety
你选择离开你的兴奋，进入焦虑的状态

Because you have fear based belief systems about yourself with regard to that situation.
因为，面对那样的情况时，你对自己有基于恐惧的负面信念

Sometimes you will leave that and go back into excitement.
有时候，你会离开焦虑，回到兴奋中

We would simply suggest that you remain in excitement, instead of shifting into anxiety.
我们只是建议你，保持在兴奋的状态，而不是转变为焦虑

But if you do shift into anxiety, ask yourself why
但如果你真的变焦虑了，问问自己：“为什么？”

What is the belief you have about yourself in that situation that makes you go into anxiety that makes you afraid of something might happen that you do not prefer.
“在那种情况下，你内心有什么关于自己的信念，让你变得焦虑，使你害怕一些可能发生的你不喜好的事情呢？”

Why do you have that definition?
“你为什么有这样的定义呢？”

Do you understand?
你明白了吗？

问：
Half of it
似懂非懂

巴夏：
When you go into anxiety that's because you have a fear based belief that you think something bad is going to happen.
当你变得焦虑时，那是因为，你有一种基于恐惧的信念，让你认为，会有不好的事情发生

Why do you have that belief?
你为什么有这样的信念呢？

You have to find out why you're holding onto that belief.
你必须找出你为什么会坚持这样的信念的原因

So you can let it go.
这样，你就可以把它放下

All anxiety is the result of fear based beliefs.
所有的焦虑，都是由基于恐惧信念造成的

So you must have fear based beliefs about yourself that says that it's more likely than not that something bad is going to happen.
所以，你对自己，肯定有个基于恐惧的信念，在跟你说：很有可能会发生什么糟糕的事情

问：
好的，我明白了！谢谢您！
