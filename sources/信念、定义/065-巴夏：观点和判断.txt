问：这个问题是你能定义观察，观点，意见和判断吗？

This question is can you define observations, perspectives, opinions and judgments?



巴夏：观察是中立的，

Observations are neutral,



观点是你特定的视角，仍然可以是中立的。

Perspectives are your particular point of view that can still be neutral.



人类典型的判断方式通常会是在你的观察中加入你的意见和信念，

Judgments in the way that typically humans mean that usually goes into some sort of adding your opinion your belief,



加入可能是积极的，也可能是消极的观点。

a perspective that may or may not be positive or negative into the mix of your observation.



换句话说，判断和意见代表了你的观察经过了你特定的信念系统的过滤，

In other words, judgments and opinions represent the filtering of observations through your particular belief system,



无论你看到的是正面的还是负面的，无论你的视角是积极的还是消极的，

Whether it's positive or negative as to what it is you are observing, what your perspective of it is,



观点有时会是已经经过了你的信念系统的过滤，

Perspective can sometimes already be representative of a filtering through your belief system,



所以你才会这么看，

Which is why you have that perspective,



尽管也有中立的观点。

Although there are neutral perspectives as well.



所以基本上观察可以是中立的，

So basically observations can be neutral,



观点可以是中立的，

Perspectives can be neutral,



但是也可以代表一种信念的过滤。

But can be representative of filtering through a belief.



判断和意见通常代表了经过信念系统过滤后的观察结果，

Judgments and opinions are usually representative of filtering an observation through a belief system,



无论是积极的，中立的还是消极的。

Whether positive neutral or negative.
