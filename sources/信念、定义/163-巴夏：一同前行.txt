巴夏：一同前行
原创 巴夏Bashar 巴夏Bashar 2023-08-20 20:15


问：
Is there anything else you'd like to add to final thoughts about being here on earth and how to really have a wonderful heaven on earth experience?
关于我们在地球上，如何创造犹如“人间天堂”般的神奇人生体验，你有什么“最后的想法”，想要在此次传讯结束之前补充的吗？

巴夏：
My thoughts will never be final.
我的想法，永远不会有“最后”

We will always have something to say to be of assistance in helping to guide you into the understanding that you have all the power, you chose to be where you are.
我们总是有话可说，有话要说，从而协助你们，引导你们认识到：你拥有所有的力量，你选择了你目前的境况。

That's why you are in physical reality.
这也是你为什么来到物质世界

You knew it would serve you in some way shape or form
你知道，它肯定会以某种方式助益于你

So we would always encourage you to look for the definitions for anything that happens to be the definitions that would be the ones that would allow you to experience the service in a positive way.
所以，我们一直鼓励你们，检视自己的定义，对于所发生的任何事件，去找寻那些能带给你正面助益的定义，进而经验正面的影响。

Instead of simply falling victim to the idea that just because this is happening, something is bad, that something is wrong, that it doesn't belong in your life.
而不是以受害者自居，认为：
因为，发生了这样的事

所以，情况很糟糕

哪里出了问题

我的人生，不该这样子

……
If it's there, it’s there for a reason
如果事情发生了，那么，它的发生，是有原因的

Own it, take responsibility for it, you will be able to transform it in wondrous and magical ways that will allow you to experience a life of beautiful joy.
拥有它，对它负责，那么，你将能够以神奇的方式转变它，你的生活也将因此而充满美好与喜乐

So we will now encourage you to explore with us the upcoming exercise and meditation.
现在，我们鼓励你，与我们一同探索接下来的冥想与练习

Thank you Bashar, we love you very much.
谢谢您，巴夏！我们非常爱您！

巴夏：
Our unconditional love to you all
我们也无条件地爱给你们所有人

And allow yourselves now to move forward together.
现在，请与我们一同前进吧！
