Bashar：我们（艾莎莎妮人）没有信念
[InfiniteONE 翻译]

Q：你们所有的艾莎莎妮人都有相同的信念吗？

B：我们都拥有关于存在如何运作的一般知识，所以我们可以看到存在的本质或结构，我们向你们描述过它。（在我们与你们的互动中，）我们并没有分享哲学观点; 我们实际上是在向你们描述实相如何运作的指示条目。我们可以看到存在的结构，所以我们只是在向你们描述我们的观察。它真的不是信念。

Q：所以你们真的没有任何信念？

B：因各种不同的理由，如果我们希望拥有某种必须与一个信念系统相伴随的体验，我们会有暂时使用的信念系统。不过，它真的超越了信念，因为它实际上更多的是知晓，而知晓高于或超出相信。

Q: Do you all have the same beliefs?

B: We all have the same general knowledge of how Existence works, so, we can see the nature the structure of Existence, that was describing to all of you. We are not sharing a philosophical point of view; we are actually describing an instruction menu for how reality works. We can see the structure of Existence, so we are just describing an observation to you. It is not really a belief.

Q: So you don't really have any belief?

B: We do have belief system that work temporarily for a variety of different reasons, if we wish to have certain experience that a belief system have to go hand in hand with, but really goes beyond that, because it really more about knowing, which is above or beyond believing.
