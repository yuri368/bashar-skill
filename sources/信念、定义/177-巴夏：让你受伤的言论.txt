巴夏：让你受伤的言论

传讯时间：2024年12月21日
标题：Shifting Realities
问：
As you've encouraged us to start conversations with those on the other side, I've been following my highest excitement by trying to engage meaningfully with family members whose views I deeply disagree with, especially when those views seem rooted in prejudice or hatred.
正如你鼓励我们与持不同观点的人展开对话那样，我怀揣着满腔热情，努力跟那些与我的观点迥异的家人进行深入且有意义的交流，尤其是当他们的观点有着似乎根深蒂固的偏见或仇恨之时

I genuinely want to respond with curiosity and understand their perspective.
我真的很想带着好奇做出回应，并理解他们的观点

But my reactions of sadness, pain, and ultimately disgust often show on my face and shut down the conversation.
但我脸上的悲伤、痛苦，以及最终的厌恶之情，总是不自觉地显露出来，这让对话戛然而止。

I recognize these emotions stem from my own thoughts and beliefs, but I'm unsure of how to identify the limiting beliefs that are preventing me from fostering genuine dialogue and connection.
我认识到，这些情绪源于我自己的想法和信念，但我不确定如何识别那些阻碍我建立真诚对话和连接的限制性信念

How can I shift my perspective to keep the conversation open, despite the hurt that I feel.
我该如何转变自己的视角，使对话得以继续展开，尽管我感觉受到了伤害

巴夏：
The hurt you feel is based on the idea that you are judging yourself, based on what they are saying, that you feel that what they are saying, you're taking personally.
你感受到的伤害，源于你根据他们的话来评判自己，你觉得他们的话是针对你个人的

As if it disqualifies or invalidates the beliefs that you have, just because they have contrary beliefs.
就好像他们在否定、贬损你的信念，仅仅是因为他们的信念与你想反

So don't take it personally, allow yourself to understand that they can make whatever statement they wish to make. It has nothing to do with invalidating the beliefs that you hold to be true for your reality.
所以，不要往心里去，告诉自己：他们可以发表任何他们想发表的言论。他们的言论，并不能否定你所坚信的你的世界的真相

So the only reason you feel hurt is that in some way, shape or form, you are thinking that what they are saying is invalidating the beliefs that are relevant for your reality.
所以你感到受伤的唯一原因是：在某种程度上，你认为他们的话，否定了跟你现实相关的你的信念
