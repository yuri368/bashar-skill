原创 Ashitaka 无限宇宙之爱 2016-11-26

Judgement is invalidating that which you do not prefer. Whenever you judge anything, by focusing your attention on it, you exchange energies with it, and so you "become" that vibration.

批判是无效化你不喜欢的。每当你通过专注于批判任何事物时候，你是在和它交换能量，所以你“成为”那个振动。



 So you automatically become the very thing you disapprove of. You become that which you invalidate. Preference, on the other hand, is not judgement. Preference is exercising your ability to discern what is really "you", what excites you, and then making choices aligned with the real "you".

所以你会自动成为你不赞成的特有的事物。你将成为你无效化的事物。偏好，是在另一面，不是批判。偏好是锻炼你有能力辨别什么是真正的“你”，什么让你兴奋，然后选择与真正的“你”对齐。



 To prefer one thing does not necessitate invalidating those other things you do not prefer.You become whatever you focus your attention on. To assign any meaning to anything - either a positive meaning or a negative meaning - is to invite that into your life. 

偏好一件事物不需要无效化那些其它你不偏好的事物。你会变成你所关注的所专注的任何事物。赋予任何意义到任何事物上 -  不是一个积极的意义就是一个消极的意义-就是邀请积极或消极的事物进入你的生活中。



Being neutral about something - assigning it no meaning - is the way to not attract that reality into your life.

对某事物保持中立，不赋予它意义，是一个方法来不吸引那样的实际情形进入你的生活中。
