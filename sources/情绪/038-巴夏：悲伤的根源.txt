巴夏：悲伤的根源

译者：Ashitaka 阿西达卡
传讯时间：2023年12月16日




问：这个人问的是悲伤的根源是什么？
And this person is asking what is the source of sadness?

这是可以避免的吗？
And can it be avoided?

我们应该为幸福而奋斗，还是只要遵循这个兴奋公式，幸福就会自然而然地来到我们身边？
Should we be striving for happiness or will it simply come to us? if we follow the formula.


巴夏：悲伤是一种与源头分离的信念和定义的结果，
Sadness is the result of a belief and a definition of disconnection from Source,

分离是，如果你明白你不会被分离，
Disconnection is the idea if you understand you cannot be disconnected,

你就不会感到悲伤。
You cannot feel sadness.


所以在为你自己、你的意识创造某些限制的体验中，
So in the idea of creating certain limitations for yourself for your Consciousness to experience,

包括分离的体验，即使你永远不可能真正与源头分离，
including the experience of disconnection, even though you can never actually be disconnected from Source,

你可以体验到这种分离是一种损失，
You can experience the idea of that disconnection as a kind of loss,

这会促进悲伤的感受。
And that can promote the experience of sadness.

但是，是的，如果你明白这个公式是如何运作的，
But, yes, if you understand how the formula works,

你精确地应用它，
And you apply it precisely,

然后基于了解事物运作的方式，你也明白了你永远不会被分离。
Then you also have the understanding based on knowing how things work that you can never be disconnected.

因此，没有理由以那种方式体验悲伤，
And thus, there is no reason to experience sadness in that way,

所以通过精确地遵循兴奋公式，
So by really precisely following the formula,

你不太可能选择去体验悲伤。
It is highly unlikely for you to choose to experience sadness.
