标题：Shifting Realities
问：

How do we exist in the here and now, the present moment and still be there for our future selves.

我们如何存在于此时此地（也就是“当下”这一刻），同时，又能为未来的自己做好准备呢？



For instance, how do we stay tuned into the present challenge and still be able to make long term, sometimes difficult choices that will benefit us later, but may be difficult in the now.

例如，我们如何才能保持对当下挑战的关注，同时又能做出那些长远的、有时显得艰难的选择，这些选择在未来将对我们有益，但在“当下”却可能充满挑战



巴夏：

Again, the challenges that you face in the now determine what you will experience in the next Now, which is what you call the future.

你“现在”所面临的挑战，决定了你在下一个“现在”的经历，也就是你们所谓的“未来”



Stay present with the challenge.

与你的挑战，同在“当下”这一刻



Again, follow the formula in dealing with any challenge.

按照“激情公式”的步骤，去应对任何挑战



It will allow you to set up the vibration of patterns that will allow you to experience the next present Now in the way that will continue to move you forward in a beneficial way.

这将调整并设定你的能量振动模式，让你的下一个“当下”体验有益于你，并持续推动你朝着更有益的“当下”前进



That is what you do.

这就是你要做的
