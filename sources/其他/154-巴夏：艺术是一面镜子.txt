巴夏：艺术是一面镜子
原创 巴夏Bashar 巴夏Bashar
 2024年08月08日 20:22 福建
问：
I'm a dancer.
我是一名舞者
And my life is dancing.
我的生活，就是舞蹈

I teach dance. I perform.
我教舞蹈，也表演舞蹈

And I notice in the world, the arts in any way shape or form are very a necessity are very well here in on this earth.
我注意到，在这个世界上，任何形式的艺术，都是非常必要的，也都是非常重要的

And I was wondering what the meaning of arts in any shape or form is on an earthly level or on a cosmic level.
我想知道，不论何种形式的艺术，在地球层面或宇宙层面上，它们的意义是什么？

巴夏：
Art is a basis for self reflection, for communication to put one in touch with oneself and how one responds to the art.
艺术是自我反思（反射）的基础（途径），也是沟通的桥梁。它能让人与自己内在连接，并看到自己对艺术的反应

So art is a mirror.
所以，艺术是一面镜子

问：
And do you have arts in other?
你们星球也有艺术吗？

巴夏：
Absolutely. Many different kinds?
当然有，我们有很多不同类型的艺术

I myself am a sculptor, as well as being a first contact specialist.
我自己是一名雕刻家，同时也是一名第一类接触专家

问：
How does that visualize? What does that look like?
你雕刻的作品，看起来像什么样？

巴夏：
There are many different things it can look like.
看起来，它们有很多种不同的样子

We can form certain materials spontaneously that are nano materials.
我们的雕刻过程，可以自发的生成某些纳米材料

What you would call a kind of metallic crystalline substance that can be formed into many different shapes.
用你们的说法，就是一种金属晶体物质，它们可以变化成（形成）许多不同的形状

And the idea is that certain sculptures can be made so that they change shape with the person that is observing it to literally reflect what is going on in that person in their observation of it.
我们制作出的某些雕刻作品，可以随着观察者的改变而改变形状，真实地映照出观察者在观看时自身的变化

So it literally functions like a kind of symbolic mirror.
它们的作用，完全就像是一面“象征镜”

问：
So if I'm understanding this correctly, the art that is being shaped by what the person is looking at?
如果我理解得正确的话，这种艺术品，会被看着它的人所重塑吗？

巴夏：
是的

问：
Wow that's beautiful.
哇，那真是太奇妙了！

巴夏：
Yes，we think so.
是的，我们也这么认为

问：
I hope I can experience that.
我希望，有机会我能体验一下

巴夏：
There are forms that you can create that will react and respond to people in different ways. Some of it may include electronics and so on and so forth.
你们可以创造的形式有很多，都可以实现对人做出不同的反应和回应，其中一些，可能要加入了电子元器件等东西

Until such time as you actually develop physical materials that will respond the way I am telling you ours respond.
直到你真正开发出 能像我所说的那样对观察者做出反应的物质

But there are many ways to do it.
但实现的方法有很多

So use your creative imagination and see what you can come up with.
所以，请发挥你们的创造性想象力，看看你们能想出什么点子

Certainly dance itself is something that can be interactive in a two way street,
当然，舞蹈本身就是一种可双向互动的艺术形式

So that the response of the audience can change the way that you dance in the moment if you are receptive to the mood of the audience.
所以，如果你对观众的情绪有所感知，那么，你就可以通过观众的反应，实时改变你的舞姿或舞步

问：
That's a very nice inspiration.
这是一个非常好的启发

巴夏：
All right. See what you can do with that.
那很好！看看你能用它做些什么

问：
Yes. Thank you so much, Bashar.
好的，非常感谢您，巴夏！

巴夏：
You are so welcome. Anything else.
你太客气了。还有别的问题吗？

问：
No, that's it for today.
没了，今天就问到这

巴夏：
All right.Then happy dancing.
好的！那么，祝你跳舞愉快！

问：
Thank you so much.
非常感谢！
