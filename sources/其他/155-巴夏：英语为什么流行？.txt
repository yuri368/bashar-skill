问：
I have a question around the English language.
我有一个关于英语的问题

Now there are over 7,000 languages, spoken languages in the world, and the English language happens to be the most prevalent I understand
据我所知，目前世界上在用的语言，超过7000多种，而英语，则是最流行的语言

I wanted to know from your perspective, what the language Where it originates from. And what the essence is? Is there a magic to it? Why is it so prevalent?
我想知道，从你的角度看，英语这门语音，它起源于哪里？它的本质是什么？它有什么魔力吗？为什么它如此流行呢？

巴夏：
There is a magic to every language
每一种语言，都有其魔力

But the reason that it is so prevalent is because it is a hybrid language formed of many other languages
但英语之所以如此流行，是因为 它是由许多其他语言混合而成的语言

And thus represents the coming together and the hybridization and moving forward of the human race as the 6th hybrid race.
它象征着人类与其他种族相聚、混血、不断地向前发展，并进化成第六个混合种族

So it is an amalgam of different languages
所以，它是不同语言的融合体

So that everyone can participate. Everyone can feel some degree of familiarity with it.
每个人都可以参与进来，每个人对它都有一定程度的熟悉感

It is going toward the idea of a universal language.
它正朝着通用语言的方向发展

It is still in its interim state, but basically it is building toward that.
目前，它仍处于过渡的状态，但基本上，它是朝着这个方向发展的

很显然，语言是通过空气来发声和传播的

Therefore, that is the primary element that carries the waves of sound to each other throughout the world.
空气，是语言所产生的声波，能够在世界传播的主要载体

And therefore, that is the focal point elemental speaking of the concept of language.
因此，从元素角度上看，空气是构成语言的重要元素
