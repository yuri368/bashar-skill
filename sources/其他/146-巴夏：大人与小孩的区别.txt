巴夏：大人与小孩的区别
原创 巴夏Bashar 巴夏Bashar
 2024年08月14日 20:13 福建
问：
When I first met you, I felt like you were my daddy.
当我第一次见到你时，我感觉你就像我爸

And I'm really, really learning how to not feel that way anymore.
现在，我则是努力学习让自己不再有那样的感觉

巴夏：
Why?
为什么呢？

问：
Why what?
什么为什么？

巴夏：
Why not feel that way?
为什么不再有那样的感觉呢？

问：
Because you say we have to be equal before we can meet you.
因为你说过，我们需要达到“平等”状态才能相遇

巴夏：
I understand.
我明白

But you don't forget that your biological father is your father, even as you become an adult.
但是，即使你成年了，你也没有忘记你亲生父亲是你的父亲

But you don't rely on them in the same way.
只是你不再像以前那样依赖他罢了

Is that what you meant?
你是这个意思吗？

问：
Anyway, but you told me to eat pineapple side for breakfast and I thought, Oh, I can tell Bashar I had pineapple, and I thought that's stupid.
之前，你跟我说吃点菠萝当早餐。后来，我就想：“噢！我可以告诉巴夏我吃了菠萝皮”，然后，我又想“噢！这也太蠢了”

巴夏：
Well, it's not stupid. But it is very childlike.
这并不愚蠢，只是很像小孩的行为

But not necessarily in a bad way.
但不一定就是不好的

Unless you are putting connotations in it that are negative.
除非你给它赋予的涵义，是负面的

问：
Well, is it negative to want your approval?
那么，想要得到你的认可，这是负面的吗？

巴夏：
Yes.
是的

Because you don't need it.
因为你并不需要

Because you already have it.
因为，你已经有了

Let me put it this way.
我这么说吧

The child seeks approval because the child doesn't know the child is already approved.
孩子寻求认可，那是因为孩子不知道自己已经被认可了

The adult doesn't need to seek approval because the adult approves themselves and knows that it will always be reflected.
成年人不需要寻求认可，因为成年人认可自己，并且知道这种自我认可总会在外界得到反映

We recently spoke of the idea that people on your planet pray for things, ask for things: “Please give us this. Please give us that.”

最近，我们谈到了这样一个观点，就是，你们星球上的人会祈祷，你们祈求某些东西：“请给我们这个！”，“请给我们那个！”

As if it's not already being given, which it is.
像是它们还没给你们，实际上，它们已经给到你们了

Prayer is not for the purpose of asking for things you don't have.
祈祷的目的，不是为了寻求你所没有的东西

Prayer is for the purpose of putting you in the proper state of gratitude.
祈祷的目的，是为了让你进入适当的感恩状态

So that you recognize you already have been given these things.
这样你就能认识到，这些东西已经给到你了

And when you're in that state, you will see that they already exist.
当你处在这种状态下，你就会发现它们已经存在了

You will experience that they already exist through what you call manifestation.
你通过所谓的“显化”，而体验到它们已经存在

But manifestation doesn't mean you didn't have it and were just given it.
但“显化”并不意味着你之前没有，不是说给了你之后才显化出来

You are always being given.
（因为）你总是被给予的

You are always being loved.
你总是被爱着的

You are always being supported.
你总是被支持着的

The fact that you may not feel that's the case is because you are not the vibration that is appropriate to sense it, to perceive that you're already always being given what you think you need to ask for.
事实上，你之所以没这样的感觉，那是因为，你没有处在这种恰当的振动频率下，以至于你无法感知到“凡你所求的，必定已给予你”

So the adult doesn't ask for approval.
所以，成年人不会寻求认可

The adult simply goes into the state of gratitude that it is already approved.
成年人只是进入到那种 已经被认可的感恩状态

That's the difference between a child and an adult.
这就是孩子与成年人的区别

Does that help?
这对你有帮助吗？

问：
OK, thank you.
好的，谢谢！
