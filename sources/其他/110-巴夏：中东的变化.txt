Okay, and this is the last question we have time for.You mentioned that the conflict in the Middle East will escalate before it gets better and that it will take a few years until there will be an event that is a breaking point.Might that event be the 2026 or 2027 contact event that you have predicted?

巴夏：这是事件的一部分，但事情不会，我们可以说，马上平息，这需要一些时间。在那个地区和你们星球的其他地区，事情已经发生了很长一段时间。尽管它将开始改变事情。这需要一些时间，人们会有一个过程来习惯可能发生的其他事情，其他对每个人都会更好的可能性。

It's part of it,but things will not,shall we say,level out right away.It will take some time.Things have been going on in that area and other areas of your planet for quite some time.Even though it will start to change things.It will take some time and there will be a process for people to get used to other things that can happen,other probabilities that can be better for everyone.

所以，这仍然需要一些时间。再次，这全都是你们2020到2050年之间被称为人类重置及其进化路径的一部分。但随着时间的推移，事情会逐渐平息。这不会在一夜之间发生，但对于那些在这条轨道上的人来说，这将会发生。

So, it will still take some time.Again, it's all part of what has been termed the reset of humanity and their evolutionary path between your years of 2020 and 2050.But things will smooth out over time.It will not happen overnight,but it will happen for those on that track.
