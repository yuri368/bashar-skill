So, yes, it's a sacred circuit that can help rewire the brain when focused on in a positive energy, in a positive state, to understand how to blend with and align with the creative energies of the universe, of all that is.But it has to be used that way. Obviously, the way the Nazis used it was a misunderstanding and a corruption.

这是一种原来的目的，存在于许多古老文明中，如藏族、美洲原住民等。他们理解它的原始含义，并以原来的方式使用它。如果你愿意，你也可以。

It's an original intention, which exists in many ancient civilizations, Tibetan, Native American, so on and so forth.They understood its original meaning and used it in its original way. So you can, too, if you wish.
