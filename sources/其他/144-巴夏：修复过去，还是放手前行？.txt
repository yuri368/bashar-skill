巴夏：修复过去，还是放手前行？
原创 巴夏Bashar 巴夏Bashar
 2024年12月30日 20:35 福建
传讯时间：2024年12月21日
标题：Shifting Realities

问：
Since we are constantly shifting between realities, if we feel we've given away our energy, become disconnected, or fallen out of balance? Is it necessary to go back and fix or clarify the situation with the person or circumstance involved?
既然我们在不同现实之间不断地转换，那么，如果我们感到能量流失、联系中断，或失去平衡，那我们是否有必要回去与相关人事物修复关系，或是澄清事实呢？

Or can we simply reclaim our energy, reconnect within ourselves, and move forward?
还是说，我们只要重拾自己的能量，连接自己的内在，然后继续前进即可？

In general, when is it best to address the past versus letting go and moving on?
一般来说，我们最好什么时候该修复过去，什么时候该放手前行呢？

巴夏：
It depends on the circumstance and the situation,
这要看具体情况

If you feel it is important for you, in some way, shape or form to clarify your intentions with another person in terms of what happened. That's all well and good.
对于发生过的某件事，如果你觉得，有必要通过某种方式，向某人说明你的初衷，那也没什么不好

If you're not necessarily able to do that, or it's really not necessarily relevant for you to do that, you can simply learn the lesson and move forward.
如果你没法做到，或者没必要这么做，那你只要学习其中的功课，然后继续前行即可

Either can work.
二者皆可！
