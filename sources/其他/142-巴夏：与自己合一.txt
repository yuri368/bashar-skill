巴夏：与自己合一
原创 巴夏Bashar 巴夏Bashar
 2024年08月06日 22:22 福建

问：
I would really love to experience oneness
我真的很想体验“合一”的感觉

And I don't know if there is a specific meditation that I could do or some kind of principle, mind training I could do
我想知道有没有什么特定的冥想我可以练习，或者什么心智训练的方式、方法我可以做的？

（这时候，提问者家里的狗狗在汪汪叫）

巴夏：
It sounds like your dog has the information that you need
听起来，似乎你的狗狗，有你需要的信息

Oneness.
合一（与狗叫声“汪汪”发音一致）

You say you want to experience oneness.
你说，你想体验合一

And I said it sounds like your dog has the information that you need.
而我说，听起来你的狗狗有你需要的信息

问：
He probably does.
它可能真的有

巴夏：
So oneness
说到，“合一”

What is your definition of experiencing oneness？
你对“体验合一”的定义，是什么？

What do you imagine that that would feel like？
你想象到的感觉，是什么样的？

问：
I imagine it would feel peaceful and calm and without conflict
我想象的是，和平宁静，没有冲突

巴夏：
Peaceful and calm.
和平宁静

So anything else?
还有别的吗？

What does oneness mean to you?
合一，对你来说，意味着什么？

问：
It means going with the flow and being helpful to people and to myself and
意味着，顺应生命的流动，帮助他人和自己

Just basically just smooth living.
就是平稳顺畅地生活

巴夏：
All right
好的

Being of service in that way.
以那样的方式，去服务

So you have just listed a bunch of things that are relatively physically practical.
既然你列出了一堆相对实际可行的事情

What is it that prevents you from behaving that way？
那么，是什么阻碍你以那样的方式行事呢？

问：
And I guess it's my own attitude.
我想，是我自己态度的问题

巴夏：
Yes.
是的

So if you want to experience oneness, you have to be one with yourself.
所以，如果你想体验合一，那么，你必须与自己合一

You have to be at peace with yourself
你必须与自己和平相处

And not define things in a negative way when you come upon a challenge
并且，在遇到挑战时，不要以负面的方式去定义事物

So you have to approach them with oneness, with connectivity, with communication.
你必须以合一、连接、沟通的方式，去面对它们

You have to approach them in a peaceful way.
你必须以和平的方式，去应对它们

You have to have a peaceful energy within yourself and a flow within yourself
你内在必须拥有的和平的能量，并以和平的方式流动

So that you can experience going with your flow when a challenge arises
这样的话，当挑战出现时，你就能体验到“顺应自己能量的流动”

Because challenges help you grow.
因为，挑战帮助你成长

They're there for a positive reason.
它们的存在，是有积极正面原因的

Look at them that way and approach them with your flow.
以这样的态度看待它们，并以你的能量流动去应对它们

How can this serve me?
（问自己）“这如何能为我所用？”

How can I use this challenge to serve others in a positive way?
“我如何能利用这个挑战，以积极的方式，为他人服务呢？”

Dogs are a symbol of service on your planet
在你们星球上，狗狗是“服务”的象征

So be of service in any challenge that you face
所以，对于任何挑战，你都要以服务的心态去面对

But approach it from a sense of oneness, from a sense of connectivity, from a sense of peace within yourself.
但是，在你内心，你需要有与它们“合一”的感觉，跟它们“连接”的感觉，跟它们“和平相处”的感觉

问：
Thank you so much Bashar.
巴夏，非常感谢您！

巴夏：
You're welcome.
不客气！

Will that help you？
这对你有帮助吗？

问：
Yes. Thank you so much.
是的，非常感谢你！

Good day
祝你有美好的一天

巴夏：
Thank you.Good day to you too.
谢谢！你也是！
