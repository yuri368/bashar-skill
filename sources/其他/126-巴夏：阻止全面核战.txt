巴夏：阻止全面核战
原创 阿奎亚 巴夏说
 2024年11月17日 06:00 辽宁
前言

全球热核战争的想法会撕裂维度结构，进入其他现实，这是你们不被允许做的。

                 译者：阿奎亚 Aquia

              传讯时间：2022年2月26日

问：还有一个后续问题，那就是外星人会阻止全面的核战争吗？他们是如何做到这一点的？

There was a follow-up question also, which was, will the ETs prevent full-scale nuclear war, and how can they do that?

巴夏：正如我们多次说过的那样，你们星球上拥有核导弹的军事基地已经发生了许多演示。我们的飞船在那里通过悬停并延伸电磁光束，关闭发射代码，消除发射代码，打乱发射代码，以证明虽然仍有可能发生有限的核打击，在你们世界的某个地方仍有可能会发生一次小规模的核打击。

As we have said many times, there have been many demonstrations given over the military bases on your planet that harbor nuclear missiles.Where our crafts have, by hovering over and extending their electromagnetic beams, shut down the launch codes, erased the launch codes, scrambled the launch codes, to demonstrate that while it might still be possible for there to be a limited nuclear strike, A small one somewhere on your world.

图片


全球热核战争的想法会撕裂维度结构，进入其他现实，这是你们不被允许做的。所以这将在更大的范围内被阻止，尽管在维度间没有这种影响的较小规模仍然可以被允许，因为你们仍然必须自己决定你们希望在你们的世界上体验什么。但更大的核战争表达，是的，是不被允许的。并将被阻止。

The idea of global thermonuclear war would rip through the fabric of dimensions to other realities, and this you are not allowed to do.So this will be prevented on a larger scale, even though smaller scales that will not have that effect interdimensionally can still be allowed, because you still have to make up your own minds about what you wish to experience on your world.But the larger expression of it, yes, is not allowed. And will be prevented.
