问：老巴，我的第一个问题和声明是关于神圣女性特质，

Bashar, my first question and a statement concerns Divine feminity,


当地球上的女性探索和发展她们的女性天性时，

When women on earth explore and develop their feminine nature,


她们在寻找她们作为女性在地球上最好的神圣表达，

They search for their best Divine expression as women on earth,


你如何定义“神圣的女性特质”这个词？

How would you define the term "Divine feminity"?


你能给我们多解释一下吗？

And would you like to explain for us a little bit more about it?


巴夏：神圣的女性特质是一种平衡，是一种开放、接纳、接受、养育和支持的意愿，

Divine femininity is a balancing and a willingness to be open to be receptive to receive to be nurturing to support,


比喻地说，在某种意义上让人类的能量握在你的手中，并渴望它向前扩展和成长，

to allow to hold, in a sense metaphorically, in your hands the energy of humanity and the desire for it to move forward to expand and to grow,


即便一个孩子的出生也被赋予了成长和扩展的能力，

Even as a child is born and given the ability to grow and expand,


所以它是关于诞生，提供支持的诞生的能量，使人类进化成一个更大、更扩展的形式。

So it is about the birthing the supporting of the energy of the birthing of the evolution of humanity into a larger form a more expanded form.


愿意支持这些努力，

The willing to support those Endeavors,


愿意在男性能量中寻求平衡，为创造和体验这种平衡提供机会，

The willingness to seek balance within the masculine energy to provide opportunities for that balance to be created and experienced,


这样所有人都能在和谐与和平中前进。

So that all may move forward in Harmony and peace.
