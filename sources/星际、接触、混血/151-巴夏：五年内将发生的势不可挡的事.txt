巴夏：五年内将发生的势不可挡的事
2025.2.25
翻译整理：飞翔的外星人Jubilee


节选自2025.2.22《Bashar：Expect The Unexpected》




问： 

就算情况再糟，我们还有可能外星接触吗？ 有人问了这个问题。
And no matter how bad things seem to look, is contact still a possibility?This person is asking. 

巴夏：

正如我们所说，外星公开接触是势不可挡的、必然的。只是关于接触的风格、具体如何发生，取决于你们星球上的状况。但在未来五年内，接触是必然会发生的。不管什么原因，它都会发生。但接触的方式会因你们星球的状况而异。
Contact, as we have said, is inevitable.It is just a matter of the style of the contact, the way in which it's going to occur based on what's going on on your planet.But contact at this point within the next five years is inevitable. For one reason or another, it will happen.But the way it happens will be different depending on what's going on on your planet.

问：

那么，这是否意味着任何以及所有我目前能看到的，正通过不同分裂棱镜前往两个不同地球的人们，都会在接下来以他们自己选择的方式体验公开接触？从2024年9月开始的五年内？
And this is sort of along those lines: Does it mean any and all people I can visually perceive now in all different splitting prisms heading towards two different Earths are all going to experience their own chosen way of open contact in the next five years since September 2024?

巴夏： 

不，有些人根本不会有“接触”这个概念。如果那是他们选择的道路，但这种不体验接触的情况非常罕见。
No, there will be some people who will not experience the idea of contact at all if that is their path. This is few and far between.

但即便如此，仍然可能有人不会体验到任何接触。不过，你们星球上的大多数人都会以某种方式体验到接触。
But nevertheless, it is still possible to experience no contact. But the majority of people on your planet will experience contact in one way, shape, or form.

问： 

下一个问题是：加拿大会变成美国的第51个州吗？
And this question is: Will Canada become the 51st state?

巴夏： 

当然不会。
Of course not.

问： 

下一个问题是：我们将如何接收我们人类历史的信息？会通过心灵感应还是视觉媒体？
And this question is: How will we receive the downloads of our human history? Will it be telepathic or by forms of visual media?

巴夏： 

你们会收到全息图。

You will receive holograms.



问：

能详细说明一下吗？
Can you elaborate?

巴夏：

你们会收到一些设备，这些设备会全息投影地展现你们的历史——从你们的历史全息投影出（这里有个歧义：是播放历史还是从历史中播放出来？）。
You will receive devices in which holographic projections will illustrate the idea of your history. Will project the idea of images holographically from your history.

问：

听起来真不错。
Sounds pretty exciting. 


巴夏：

是的。

Yes.


问： 

人工智能与外星人来地球有关吗？你们外星人会来协助发展人类的人工智能吗？
Does AI have anything to do with ETs coming to Earth? And are you here to help parent it?

巴夏： 

某种程度上，外星人当公开接触发生后，将会协助指导创造人工智能以造福人类。是的。
To some degree, extraterrestrials, when open contact occurs, will help guide the idea of the creation of AI to the benefit of humanity. Yes.
