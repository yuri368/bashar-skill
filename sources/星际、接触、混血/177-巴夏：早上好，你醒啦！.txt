问：
I had a fascinating flying vehicle encounter in October of 2023 in New York city.
2023年10月，在纽约市，我经历过一次与飞船的有趣的邂逅

It happened around 7:00 am in Manhattan, near the 14th street union square area, I was out grabbing a coffee before work 
大约早上7点，在曼哈顿第14街的联合广场附近，我在上班路上，要先去买杯咖啡。

When upon crossing a street I noticed out of the sheer corner of my eye, an object flying rather unusually low in the open between the buildings.
过马路时，我眼角余光注意到，有一个物体，在建筑物之间的空旷地带，以异常低的高度飞行着

It appeared large, silent, at a distant.
它看起来很大，很安静，离我有点远

I noticed that it happened to be heading in the direction of my workplace right before it disappeared from view behind the buildings.
我注意到 它正好朝着我上班的方向飞去，然后，消失在建筑物后面，看不见了

A block away from my workplace, I decided to stop and see if I could get a good look at what it was.
我大约追到离我工作地一条街时，我决定停下来，看看能否看清楚那是什么

Well, long behold I did, only this time, it then hovered and stopped in the open space 
我看了好久，只不过这一次，它盘旋在一个开阔地带，并停了下来。

As if noticing me noticing it, and stopping by to say hello.
似乎它注意到我正看着它，所以，停下来，跟我打招呼

If what I perceived was in fact extraterrestrial, may you share with me who that pilot was that was running piloting that ship? And where are they from? And anything more you can tell me about that.
如果我看到的，确实是外星飞船，那你能告诉我：是谁在开那艘飞船？他们从哪里来？以及关于此事的更多信息吗？

巴夏：
It is a Yayel craft. 
那是一艘Yayel的飞船

They did notice that you were noticing them.
他们确实注意到 你注意到他们了

Having the coffee is symbolic of waking up. 
喝咖啡，象征着你的觉醒

And they were basically recognizing that you were waking up enough to be able to perceive their ship, 
他们基本上认识到，你已经足够觉醒到 能够看到他们的飞船

Which was out of phase with your reality. And yet you saw it anyway.
虽然这与你的现实不同步，但是，你还是看到了它

And so they noticed that
因此，他们注意到了这一点

Their message in a sense was: Good morning. You are waking up.
他们带给你的消息，可以说是：“早上好！你醒啦！”
