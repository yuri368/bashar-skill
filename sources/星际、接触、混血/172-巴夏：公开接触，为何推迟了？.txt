问：
My contact crystal broke.
我的爱莎莎尼接触水晶碎了。

Is that a symbolic representation of something？
这是否有什么象征意义呢？

I still feel my heartbeat when I do the meditation on it.
当我拿着它做接触冥想时，我仍能感觉到自己的心跳

巴夏：
In that sense, it doesn't really mean that much.
从这个意义上讲，它并没有多大的象征意义。

The idea of the contact crystal breaking can be slightly a permission slip recognition that the idea of open contact has been slightly postponed.
接触水晶的破碎，多少可以看做是一个“自我意识许可”的征兆，让你认识到公开接触的时间“稍微”推迟了点

But that’s all. 
但也仅此而已

Don't worry about it.
别担心

问：
Can you tell us why it's been postponed？
你能告诉我们为什么被推迟了吗？

巴夏：
I already did, didn't I?
我已经说过了，不是吗？

Your reactions to the comments that we were made act as a barometer that tell us whether you are or not ready for contact.
你们对我们言论的反应就像是一个评估指标，让我们可以判断你们是否准备好进行接触。

So there is still a little more work to do.
所以，还需要多做一些工作

There is still a little more clarification to be had.
还需要澄清一些事情

And this is why we encourage all of you to start talking to one another, regardless of your belief system.
这就是为什么 我们鼓励你们开始相互交流，不论你们的信念系统为何

Do that work, and the nature of open contact will become more positive.
做好这项工作，那么，公开接触的性质，就会变得更为积极正面

Refuse to do the work, stay sequestered in your differences and divisiveness, open contact will take on a different flavor and a different color.
如果拒绝做这项工作，仍然固守各自的分歧和分裂，那么，公开接触就会带上不同的风味，呈现出不同的色彩

It will still happen.
它仍然会发生

But you may prefer the version of open contact that is more representative of coming together than being divisive.
只是，你们可能更喜欢那种象征着“团结”，而非“分裂”的公开接触

That's all we will say about that.
关于这个问题，我们就说这么多
