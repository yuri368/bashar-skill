巴夏：团结，而非对抗
原创 巴夏Bashar 巴夏Bashar
 2024年12月19日 00:01 福建
巴夏：
So create a new reality.
创造一个新的世界吧！

You can shift, you're still splitting to new realities, you are still moving, still shifting through different parallel versions of earth.
你们可以转变，你们仍然在分裂，进入新的世界中。你们仍然在前进，仍然在不同版本的地球中穿行

But you can certainly do that with more togetherness than divisiveness.
但你们却可以更加团结地前行，而非分裂地相互对抗

We would love to welcome all of you, not that we have to. But we would love to welcome all of you and introduce all of you to your galactic family.
我们非常乐意欢迎你们所有人，并向你们介绍你们的银河系家人（并不是说我们一定得这么做）

But you're the ones in charge.
但决定权在你们手中

Remember, fear and negativity are not conducive to open contact.
请记住：恐惧和负面不利于公开接触

That’s all we will say.
这就是我们要说的全部

And we are not pointing fingers at anyone side.
我们并不是在指责任何一方

You both have an abundance of fear and negativity. 
你们双方都有大量的恐惧和负面

Let it go.
放下它们吧

Talk to one another.
彼此交谈

Support each other.
相互支持

Explore each other.
互相了解（探索）

Find the deeper issues in each other that you can all agree on.
找出你们彼此之间能达成一致的深层次问题

Recognize, recognize in each other's character, what is representative of a willingness to be a true person. And what is representative of being something out of alignment.
洞察彼此，细心辨别在各自性格中，哪些特征透露出渴望成为真正人的意愿，而哪些特征，又暗示着与真正人的本质有所偏离

So discern. But work together to do so.
所以，用心分辨，也要一起去做

Again, there's a lot happening you are unaware of on many different levels.
再说一次，在诸多不同的层面上，有许多事情正悄然发生，而你们却对此却毫无察觉

Come together, find out what that is.
团结起来，一起去找出那是什么

Make a declaration, a declaration of true independence.
（然后）做个声明，宣布你们的真正独立

Move forward together.
（并）一同前进
