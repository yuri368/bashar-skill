问：
How do your people address the problem of scarcity or scarce resources?
你们星球的人，是如何应对资源匮乏或资源稀缺的问题的？

Here, our economists have concluded that capitalism is the best method for allocating scarce resources. But this method seems very problematic in its current stages.
在地球上，我们的经济学家得出的结论是，资本主义是分配稀缺资源的最佳方式，然而，在现阶段，这种方式似乎存在诸多问题

巴夏：
Why would you assume that we have a scarcity of resources?
为什么你们会认为我们存在资源匮乏的问题呢？

We can travel the stars.
我们都能进行星际旅行了

There is an abundance of just about anything out here.
在浩瀚的宇宙中，几乎任何资源都是无穷无尽的。

There is no scarcity.
根本不存在所谓的“匮乏”

This is one of the reasons why it's so important for earth to become a member of the galactic community so that you can understand that there is an abundance of everything for all.
这也是地球要加入银河联盟的重要原因之一，这样你们就会明白：万物皆丰盈，众生齐共享。

There is no scarcity.
不存在“匮乏”

That's an illusion that you have created on earth.
那是你们地球人创造出的“幻象”
