前言

“让你自己拥有双重身份，即一个人被认定为地球人，另一个人被认定是星际联盟的成员”

好的。下一次，我们将发表星际联盟社会实验的第四步，这将与为你们每个愿意在星际联盟中更多地参与身份的人创造有关。

再一次，这取决于你是否参与，但我们将为你提供一些东西，让你练习将自己认定为星际联盟的成员，作为这项社会实验的一部分。

我们将描述和设计在任何需要您提供身份证明的情况下，您在社会中的互动方式。因此，我们将带你完成这个有趣的实验，开始让你自己拥有双重身份，即一个人被认定为地球人，另一个被认定是星际联盟的成员。

所以，加入我们，继续星际联盟的社会实验吧当我们提供第四步时，这与在星际联盟中声明你的身份有关。

All right.Next time we will deliver step four of the Interstellar Alliance social experiment, which will have to do with creating for each of you that are willing to participate more of an identity within the Interstellar Alliance.

Again, it is up to you to decide to participate, but we will provide you with something that will allow you to practice identifying yourself as a member of the Interstellar Alliance as part of this social experiment.

We will describe and design how this will take place in terms of your interactions in society in any situation where you are required to provide identification.So we will take you through this fun experiment to begin to allow yourself to have a dual identity, that of someone identifying as an Earth human and identifying as a member of the Interstellar Alliance.

So join us for the continuation of the Interstellar Alliance social experiment when we provide step four that has to do with claiming your identity within the Interstellar Alliance.
