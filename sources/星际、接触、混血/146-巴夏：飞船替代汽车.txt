问：你已经谈到了第七个混血种族的诞生，阿努海特人，将如何为整个银河系带来10万年的和平。为什么和平不会持续更长时间？在那之后会发生什么？

You've talked about how the creation of the seventh hybrid race, the Anu-Het,will usher in 100,000 years of peace throughout the galaxy.Why doesn't the peace last longer than that? And what happens after that?

巴夏：我不是说和平不能持续更长时间，但我要说的是在阿努海特目前的安排下，这是10万年的和平。在那之后还会发生其他事情，和平可能会继续，但它将以不同的方式在不同的独特存有群体中继续。

I'm not saying the peace cannot last longer, but what I am saying is that it is 100,000 years of peace under the present arrangement of the Anu-Het.There are other things that will happen after that, The peace may continue, but it will continue in a different way with a different grouping of individual beings.

这个想法是你们的银河系将经历一个特定的周期。这将允许不同的生命体验到新的意识周期，以及他们选择体验的新体验。所以并不是说和平不能继续，我们只是在谈论在你们可能称之为银河系目前管理下的10万年和平。

The idea is that your galaxy will go through a particular cycle.That will allow for different beings to experience new cycles of awareness, new experiences as they have chosen to experience them.So it's not that the peace can't continue, We 're simply talking about 100,000 years of peace under what you might term the present administration of the galaxy.

问：当你介绍表达UFO，当你把外星飞船引入我们的社会时，它会影响我们对汽车的使用吗？

When you introduce, says UFO, so ET ships to our society, will it affect our use of automobiles?

巴夏：在某些地区，可能会，这要看情况。最终还会有其他交通方式，当然。这将使你放下对你们使用汽车的想法正如你们当今使用它们一样，新技术的引入肯定会最终用多种交通方式取代它们。

In some areas, it may, It depends,There will be other modes of transportation, ultimately, of course.That will allow you to let go of the idea of your automobiles.as you use them today, The introduction of new technologies will certainly replace them eventually with many modes of transportation.

问：考虑到这是你在早期传讯中提到的唯一人生，你现在选择在地球上化身为达里尔是为什么？

What did you choose to incarnate on Earth as Darryl now, given that this is your only life here as mentioned in an earlier transmission?

巴夏：向地球社会传递当时最有利于向公开接触转变的信息。

To deliver information to Earth's society most conducive at the time for the shifting toward open contact.
