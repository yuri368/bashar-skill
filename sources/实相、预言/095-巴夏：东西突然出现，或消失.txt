标题：Shifting Realities

问：
This person is talking about seeing things, and then they like disappear.
这个人说,他看到一些东西，然后，它们又消失了

Bows, Christmas bows, candles, just things disappearing.
比如说，蝴蝶结、圣诞节的装饰品、蜡烛等，转眼就消失不见了

When that happens, what's the best way to interpret such events?
当这种情况发生时，最佳的解读，是什么？

巴夏：
We have already discussed this.
这个，我们已经讨论过了

The idea is that when you are shifting realities in a way where you become very aware that you're shifting, and you may see the shift in certain circumstances, like things appearing that weren't there a moment ago or things disappearing that were there a moment ago.
当你能非常觉察到你正在“转变现实”时，某些情况下，你可能看见这种转变的迹象，比如刚才没有的东西出现了，或者刚才还在的东西消失了

So this can be one of the signs that you are broadening your bandwidth of awareness in such a manner that you are proving to yourself that you are actually shifting different realities.
这是你在拓宽自己“觉察宽广度”的一个迹象，通过这种方式，你向自己证实你在不同现实之间转变
