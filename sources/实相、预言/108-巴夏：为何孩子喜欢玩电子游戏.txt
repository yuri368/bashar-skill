问：

It is about kids and technology.

关于孩子跟科技的



My children are very drawn to technology, to IPads, to phones, to videos. And I just wanted to...

我的孩子沉溺于科技, 如iPad，手机，网络视频、游戏。我是想…



What's it doing for them?

这对他们有什么好处呢？



It appears to excite them, but it also appears to disconnect them from the present moment.

这些让他们很兴奋，但同时也让他们脱离“当下”（的生活）



巴夏：

Well it's your job to allow them to learn how to balance both things

这是你的工作，你需要帮助他们学会在现实世界与虚拟世界中保持平衡，



in a way that allows them to remain grounded.

如此他们才能很好地成长。



While at the same time, being capable of multitasking on a whole other multidimensional level

他们通过这些科技、游戏的方式



Which is what in a sense, their training themselves to do,

训练自己在多维度上进行多任务处理



Because they can sense that there's a whole new reality coming,

因为他们能感知到在即将到来的新世界里，



in which those skills will be important.

这些技能是非常重要的。



And at the same time, you can allow them to invest more deeply in the idea of their connection to nature.

同时，你可经常带他们去大自然中探索，保持他们与大自然的连接。



That will allow them to remain grounded even while they exhibit more of those skills,

如此帮助他们过好现实生活，同时很好地掌握这些技能
