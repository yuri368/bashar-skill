你是否理解，改变永远不会结束呢？

问：

巴夏：
What do you think it would look like if you achieved perfection?
如果你实现了完美，那么，你觉得会是什么样？

Can you describe it to us?
你能跟我们描述一下吗？

Give us an example.
能给我们举个例子吗？

问：
I'd be out of debt.
我就不欠债了

I have lots of money.
我有很多钱

I'd have.
我会……

巴夏：
I see this is all connected to finance.
我知道了……这些都与钱有关

问：
My body would be healthy.
我身体会很健康

I be in a state of joy in ecstasy all the time.
我一直处于狂喜的状态

巴夏：
Your body would be healthy.
你身体会很健康

You would have all the money you want.
你想要多少钱，就有多少钱

Do you understand that there are different forms of abundance?
你知道，富足有多种不同的形式吗？

问：
我知道

巴夏：
真的知道？

问：
真的

巴夏：
你说说看

问：
除了金钱，还有健康……

这时候，我脑中就只想到这些

巴夏：
Health is a good answer
健康，是一个很好的答案

But outside of the exchange of money on your planet, which is a viable symbol of abundance
在你们星球上，金钱作为交换手段，确实是富足的一个可行的象征，但除此之外

Being given a gift is a form of abundance.
别人送你礼物，是富足的一种形式

Having something to trade is a form of abundance.
有东西可交易，是富足的一种形式

Synchronicity is a form of abundance.
同步性，是富足的一种形式

Imagination is a form of abundance.
想象力，是富足的一种形式

Communication is a form of abundance
交流，是富足的一种形式

Because abundance is simply the ability to do what you need to do when you need to do it.
因为富足，就是在你需要做你需要做的事情时，你就能够做到的能力

That's all it is.
仅此而已

So the idea is that if you focus only on one form, you're closing the doors through which other forms can come.
所以，如果你只关注在一种形式上，那么，你就关闭了其他富足形式能够进来的大门

You're invalidating all the other forms
你否定了所有其他形式

When the natural act is to be open to all forms of abundance
而最自然的做法，则是向所有富足的形式敞开

So that in acting on your passion, whatever forms need to be there, to join together, to create the 100 % abundance that will allow you to continue to act on your passion, can then flow together and happen and support you.
这样的话，当你在你的激情上行动，那么，任何需要出现，并能结合在一起，从而创造出100%的富足，让你可以够继续在你的激情上行动，（当你向这些富足敞开）它们就能汇聚到一起，并支持你

Maybe it will be a little bit of money.
也许，是一点点钱

Maybe someone will give you a gift.
也许，有人会送你一份礼物

Maybe your imagination will be inspired to think of a way you can do things that you hadn't thought of before that won't require as much money.
也许，你的想象力，会启发你想到什么你之前没想到的方式，让你可以不需要那么多钱，就能把事情给办了

Maybe you can communicate something to someone that they find valuable and they will support you in a certain way.
也许，你可以和别人交流一些他们觉得有价值的东西，然后，他们以某种方式支持你

You have to let all the forms of abundance come together to support you
你需要让所有的富足形式，联合起来支持你

Don't limit the idea of what abundance means.
不要限制了“富足”的定义

这对你有帮助吗？
