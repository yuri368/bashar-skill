问：

Why has it become such a need for so many people?
为什么它会变成这么多人的必需品呢？


Shouldn't adrenaline be enough?
难道肾上腺素不够吗？


巴夏：

In some senses, yes.
从某种意义上讲，是的


The idea, however, is that in nature that are certain things that are provided when and where certain limitations that have been created by the collective consciousness have created the idea of limitations within the body.
然而，实际情况是：当你们的集体意识，在你们个体体内创造出某些限制时，那么，大自然中，就会相应地提供某些物质（用以打破这些限制）


Now, these things are many times overused
现在，这些东西经常被“过度使用”


And become in that sense, abused.
甚至可以说是被“滥用”了

And people become addicted to them
因此，人们对它们“上瘾”了

Because they approach the use of these substances from the idea of having lack of worth.
因为，他们使用这些物质的根本原因，是因为缺乏自我价值（能量）

And thus then can develop an addiction to these substances.
因此，他们渐渐对这些物质产生了瘾（依赖）


But the nature of the world, the natural world, provides certain elements that can act as stimulants or substitutes for certain natural enzymes and chemicals within the body, 
然而，自然界，大自然中，提供了某些元素（物质），可以作为人体的兴奋剂，或人体某些天然酶和化学物质的替代品。


when and where certain conditions within the environments in physical reality may cause a lack thereof or a depletion thereof temporarily.
你们物质世界的环境，在某些情况下或某些时候，可能会导致这些物质的暂时缺失或消耗（于是，你们就可以使用这些天然物质）


But they are only meant as temporary stop gaps.
但它们原本只是作为“临时替代品”来使用的


你明白了吗？


问：

明白！


巴夏：

When you are in perfect balance, most of you will find that you will need many of these substances far less.

当你处于完美的平衡状态时，你们大多数人会发现，你对这些物质的需求，要少得多

They are really just there's as guides, as reminders as permission slips.

它们的存在，只是作为一个“指导者”，一个“提醒者”，一种“自我意识许可”
