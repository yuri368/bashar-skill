巴夏：过度依赖，就成“拐杖”
原创 巴夏Bashar 巴夏Bashar
 2024年12月25日 21:01 福建
问：
Is it possible to become too dependent on our permission slips such as with crystals？
我们有没可能过渡依赖所使用的“自我意识许可”，比如说水晶

I had something happen and I reached into my pocket to hold on to the crystals. And I realized I've forgotten to put them in there and started to freak out, thinking I needed the crystals to keep me in the positive.
有一次，我遇到些事情，便伸手进口袋里去摸我的水晶，却发现我忘记把它们放进去了，于是我开始惊慌失措，总觉得自己需要那些水晶，才能保持积极正面状态

Even though deep down, I know it's not true.
尽管我内心深处明白，这并非事实

巴夏：
Well, If deep down you know it's not true, then you know that the answer to your question is，
如果你内心深处清楚这不是真的，那你也就知道了问题的答案

Yes of course, anything can become an addiction from the negative side.
当然啦，从负面的角度来看，任何东西若过度使用，都有可能成为一种瘾

The idea of anything you use as a permission slip is to teach you that the power is coming from you.
任何你当做“自我意识许可”来使用的物品，其目的都是让你明白，力量源自于你自己

It's just that you happen to be using a particular device that aligns with your belief system to allow yourself to be more of who you are.
你只是恰好 使用一种与你信念系统相契合的特定物品，从而让你 更好地做真实的自己

So Yes, if you rely on it more than you need to, then yes it can become a crutch.
所以，如果你过度依赖它，那么是的，它确实会变成一根“拐杖”

It can become an addiction.
它会变成一种瘾

So The fact that you panicked when you realized you didn't have it might be a good indication that you need to start allowing yourself to wean yourself from that.
因此，当你意识到自己会因没带它而感到恐慌时，那么，这可能是一个很好的迹象，说明你需要开始让自己逐渐摆脱对它的依赖

So that you can understand more clearly that you're the one that has the power.
这样你就能更清楚地明白，拥有力量的，是你自己

And that the permission slip is just allowing you to be more aware of that.
而那个自我意识许可（水晶），只是让你清晰地认识到这一点而已

It's not coming from the permission slip.
力量不是来自“自我意识许可”

It's coming from you.
而是来自于你
