问：怎么知道自己找对了那个伴侣？
How does one know when they found that partner?

我知道我看过一个你的视频，你说关系的目的是反映给对方需要知道的东西让对方能更好地成为自己，
So I know the I've seen a video where you said that the purpose of relationships is to reflect one another what one needs to know to become more of themselves.

你怎么知道自己已经准备好并学会了那一课？
How do you know that you are the version of yourself that is ready to commit and has learned that lesson?

巴夏：如果你愿意敞开，在一个以这种方式为你服务的人面前，你会感到更自由，你会感到更轻松，
You will feel freer, you will feel lighter in the presence of a person who serves you that way, if you're willing to be open to it.

你会感觉更像你自己。
You will actually feel more like yourself.

在你们的星球上，你们在这些关系中听到过这句话，
You have heard this phrase on your planet uttered in those kinds of relationships,

人们说：在你面前我感觉更像我自己，
where people say I feel more like me in your presence,

我觉得有你在的时候，我可以表现出真实的自己。
I feel I can be expressive of who I truly am, when you're around.

这种感觉通常反映了有人在为你服务，让你更加做自己，
That kind of feeling is usually a reflection of when someone is serving you to be more of yourself,

因为他们不是在评判你，
Because they are not judging you,

他们接受真实的你，
They are accepting you for who you are,

他们接受任何你可能需要经历的过程来更清楚地发现你是谁。
And accepting whatever processes you may need to go through to discover more clearly who you are.

所以这通常被解释为在这段关系中可以自由地做自己，不管此刻那是什么意思，
And so that usually translates as feeling free to be yourself in that relationship, whatever that means at the moment,

自由地探索更多的自己，
Free to explore being more,

这是一种轻松的感觉，
It's a lightness of feeling,

这是一种轻松和自由的感觉。
It's a lightness and feeling of freedom.
