Attracting Your Soul Mate Through Completeness 

通过圆满吸引你的灵魂伴侣

	



Q:  Could you speak about soul mates? 



问：你能谈谈灵魂伴侣吗？





B:  Soul mates, all right. Now, to some degree…

B：灵魂伴侣，好吧。在某种程度上...



Q:  Oooh!

问：喔！



B:  Oooh! To some degree, in one sense, everybody is your soul mate that you find yourself interacting with at all in life, because everyone has something they can reflect to you. And if you are interacting with them, you have something you can reflect to them. And as souls you are mated in that way to learn what you need to learn from each other. 

B：哦！在某种程度上，从某种意义上说，每个人都是你的灵魂伴侣，你会发现在你生活中都在与之互动，因为每个人都有他们可以反映给你的东西。如果你与他们互动，你也有可以反映给他们的一些东西。你们以这种灵魂配对的方式来互相学习你们需要学习的东西。





The way you generally mean the idea, however, is that many times two individuals will create an agreement to meet up in a physical life to reflect to each other when they have arrived at a certain point of understanding in their lives, when they have arrived at a certain point of realizing and recognizing their own completeness.

你问的大概的意思是，然而，很多时候是当他们在人生中达到了某一领悟点，当他们达到了某一意识点并认识到自己本自圆满的时候，两个人会创建一个协议来在物质生活中偶遇以此互相反射给对方。







For recognize that many individuals in your society have for a long time thought, “Well, if ONLY I could find my soul mate, everything would be, as you say, hunky dory! Life would be complete; life would be wonderful. I would be happy.”

因为认识到，你们社会中许多人会日思夜想“好吧，如果我能找到我的灵魂伴侣，一切都会，就像你们说的那样一切安好！生活将会是圆满的，生活将会是美好的，我会很高兴的。”





But understand that an individual who does not believe that they are happy or complete before that point will usually only attract a relationship into their life to reflect a belief in their own incompleteness. When you know you are complete, when you know you are a complete individual at any given moment, you are always expanding, you are always learning, you are always growing, but at any given moment, for the idea you are being at that moment, you are perfect, you are complete -- as that idea.

但是要明白的是，一个不相信自己是幸福或圆满的人，在他们认识到那点之前，通常只会吸引一种反映了他们身上不圆满信念的关系到他们的生活中。当你知道你是圆满的，当你知道你在任何时候都是一个圆满的人，你一直在扩展，你一直在学习，你一直在成长，但在任何时刻，因为你存在于那一时刻，你就是完美的，你就是完整的——如同圆满一样。





The next moment you will be a different idea, a different, complete and perfect idea. Perhaps more expanded, perhaps more grand, but never better, just different. So when you know you are complete, then you can attract that individual into your life that you have made the agreement with, to come into your life and say, “Now I can reflect to you that you have arrived at a point where you know you are complete. So let us, as two complete individuals, share the joy of our unconditional knowingness together.” You follow me? 

下一刻你将是一个不同的想法，一个不同的、完整的和完美的想法。也许更扩展，也许更宏伟，没有比这更好的了，只是不一样了。所以当你知道你是圆满的，那么你就可以把那个你已经与其达成协议的人吸引到你的生活中，进入你的生活，并说，“现在我可以向你反射，你已经到达了一个你知道自己本是圆满的特殊时刻上。所以让我们，作为两个圆满的人，一起分享我们无条件知晓的喜悦吧。”“你明白吗？





Q:  Yes.

问：恩



B:  That is what is most represented as the idea of soul mate -- or twin flame. Sometimes they can be soul mates by association, not necessarily extensions of the exact same soul. And sometimes they can actually be counterparts or extensions of the same oversoul. You follow me? 

B：这是最能描述灵魂伴侣，或者双生火焰的解释了。有时他们可以通过联合而成为灵魂伴侣，而不一定是完全相同灵魂的延伸。有时，他们实际上可以是同一超灵的副本或者延伸。你明白了吗？



Q:  Yes, I do.

问：是的，我明白了。



B:  Does that answer your question? 

B: 这有回答你的问题吗？
