It will be titled As Above, So Below 

标题为：《上行，下效》



该句来自公元36000年前的《透特的翡翠石板》，被认为是“最接近真理”的古老智慧传承。翡翠石板上的十三句箴言是炼金术的原理。



此句原句为 “That which is below corresponds to that which is above, and that which is above corresponds to that which is below,to accomplish the miracle of the one thing.” 



简化之后就是”As above, so below, as it is within, so it is without.” 翻译成中文是 “上形，下效。存乎中，形于外。”



And we will be discussing How the fundamental energy templates of nonphysical reality express themselves into physical matter 

我们将探讨非物质实相的基本能量模板是如何显化（表达）为实体物质



And how they lay a foundation for what happens in physical reality according to the fundamental states of being that exist in the higher planes 

以及祂们是如何根据位于更高层面的“基本存在状态”，来为物质世界中所发生的事件奠定基础的



In understanding this will help you to see the relationship between physical and nonphysical reality more clearly 

理解这个的话，能够帮助你们更清楚地看清物质实相与非物质实相之间的关系



And give you an opportunity to consciously utilize that relationship and that connection between what is below and what is about, what is physical what is nonphysical in a way 

带给你机会，让你可以有意识地使用“上与下”，“物质与非物质”之间的关系与连接



then help bring clarity to your understanding of how life can be expressed, and how you can move through your life in a more powerful way by linking the nonphysical with the physical in your understanding, in your consciousness 

从而帮助你清楚的认识到“你的生命，可以很牛XX地展现”、“你的人生，可以更强而有力地迈进”，而你需要做的，就是在你的认知中，在你的意识中连接物质物质与非物质实相

And how you can connect with them and amplify and magnify your communication with trees, animals, all the beings in nature 

以及你如何与它们连接，并且放大和增强你与树木、动物等大自然中的所有存在体连接



To help reveal to yourself more of your true nature
