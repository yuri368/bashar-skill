巴夏：其他文明以及爱莎莎尼探索的主题
2025.3.21
翻译整理：飞翔的外星人Jubilee




问：

之前听您说过，地球的探索主题是将黑暗转化为光明。这让我很好奇，其他星球还有哪些可能的探索主题？还有，爱莎莎尼星人的探索主题又是什么呢？谢谢。
I've heard you say before that the theme of exploration for Earth is the transformation of darkness to light. That makes me wonder what are some of the other themes of exploration that are possible on other worlds? And also, what is the theme of exploration for the Esasani? Thank you.

巴夏：

嗯，很明显，任何你已知的主题都存在对立面。所以，有些文明的主题可能是将光明转化为黑暗。这是个很明显的主题，这与我们现在所说的地球情况截然相反（将黑暗转化为光明）。
Well, obviously, any theme that you already recognize can certainly have its opposite too. So some civilizations can be the theme of transforming lightness into dark. That's one obvious theme that is simply the opposite of what we have said about Earth at this time.


爱莎莎尼的核心理念是播种，或是传递我们分享的理念。具体而言，因此，这就像协助不同系统进化，更清晰地暴露出文明系统的主题，让他们更清晰地体验这些主题，从而根据自身主题更有意识地做出抉择。
The idea of the overall theme for the Sasani people is one of seeding or spreading the ideas that we are sharing with you specifically. So it's kind of an idea of helping evolution of different systems to expose the themes that they are experiencing to them more clearly so they can make their decisions accordingly to the themes they have chosen more consciously.

所以从某种意义上说，我们的主题是“协助透明化”。提供透明提供信息，播种和种植想法，使其萌发为更透明清晰的体验，更加自我赋权，更加有意识。
So we are, in that sense, the theme of transparencies. provide transparency, provide information seed and plant ideas that will grow into the experience of more transparency, more clarity more self-empowerment, more awareness.

我们是蜜蜂，采集百花之粉以酿蜜，将蜂蜜赠予你们。
We are the bees that create the honey from the pollen of different flowers and bring you the honey as a gift.
