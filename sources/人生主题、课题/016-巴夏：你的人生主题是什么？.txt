巴夏：你的人生主题是什么？

 2024年05月16日 22:37 辽宁
前言

“这一切都是关于转化，将黑暗转化为光明。”

                                          译者：阿奎亚 Aquia

                                  传讯时间：2011年2月12日

问：我想知道我对这种物质生活的主题是什么?

I would like to know what are my themes for this physical life.

巴夏：谢谢。让我们分析一下那个问题。

Thank you. Let's take that.

正如我们已经介绍的那样，这个概念是，当你追随自己的兴奋时，你决定探索的主题将是揭示负面信念的问题。

As we have already introduced, the idea is the themes that you have decided to explore are going to be the issues that reveal negative beliefs while you're following your excitement.

这些就是主题。所以，换句话说，如果你觉得，例如，你有一种特殊的天赋，一种特定的艺术倾向，一种创造性地做某件事的特殊愿望。

Those are the themes. So, in other words, if you feel, for example, you have a particular talent, a particular artistic bent, a particular desire to do a certain thing creatively.

图片


但你在这一过程中发现，你通过行动发现，你害怕，你有自我怀疑，你出于某种原因没有采取行动，这就是你选择探索的主题。

but you find along the way, you find by acting on that, that you are afraid, that you have self-doubt, that you are not acting on it for some reason or other, that's the theme you chose to explore.

你明白吗？这一切都是关于转化，将黑暗转化为光明。

You understand? It is all about transforming, transforming the dark into light.

这个概念的一些变体。这能回答你的问题吗？

Some variation of that idea. Does that answer your question? Thank you.

问：是的，谢谢。

Yes. Thank you. 

巴夏：不客气。

Thank you.
