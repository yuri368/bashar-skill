巴夏：似曾相识
原创 巴夏Bashar 巴夏Bashar
 2024年12月26日 21:49 福建
传讯时间：2024年12月21日
标题：Shifting Realities

问：
In this transmission, you're describing the idea of other realities bleeding through into the one we are in.
在此次传讯中，你提到了其他现实渗透进我们目前所在的现实

Can you point out some of the strongest reality bleed through events that we would recognize from modern history?
在现代历史中，你能列举一些我们明显能察觉到的、最为显著的现实交织（或渗透）事件吗？

巴夏：
Not so much historic events that we will call out.
不用说特指某些历史事件

But the idea of certain experiences, like déjà vu, can be the result of bleed through from other realities that may be experiencing something similar to what you are experiencing in your timeline.
就拿你们的某些经历来说，比如 既视感/似曾相识/déjà vu，它就是其他现实渗透进来的结果。那些现实，与你们此刻所在的时间线，经历着相似的事情。
déjà vu，法语，中文翻译为“既视感”，简单而言就是“似曾相识”，未曾经历过的事情或场景仿佛在某时某地经历过的似曾相识之感。
But maybe ahead of your timeline, the event already having happened.
但可能在你的时间线之前，这个事件，就已经发生了

And therefore, when you pick up on the idea that the event has already happened in another timeline, when your consciousness is expanded enough to do so, and become sensitive to receiving that information that has happened in the other parallel timeline.
因此，当你的意识扩展到一定程度，让你能接收到发生在其他时间线上的信息，能够觉察到这个事件在其他时间线上已经发生了

And you, in your reality, finally arrive at that moment, then you have a sense that it has happened before that you know it’s going to happen. 
而你，在自己的现实中，终于到达那个事件发生的时刻了，然后，你就感觉到这事发生过，你也知道接下来会发生什么

That's one way in which the idea of bleed throughs can express themselves in your timeline through the idea of déjà vu.
这就是“现实渗透”，在你的时间线上，以“似曾相识”的形式展现的一种方式。
