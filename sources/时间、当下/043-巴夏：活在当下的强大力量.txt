When you allow yourselves to be in the moment in the way we discussed earlier

当你能做到，像我们刚才说的那样，活在当下



And give a clear and precise definition

并对经历的每件事情，都很清楚：



to every experience for its own sake as its own experience.

“这个经历有其自身发生原因，并且是独一无二的”



You develop what you are perceiving in us at this point,

你就是在开发，你在我们身上看见的能力——坚信



which is that when we talk about these principles,

对于我们说到的这些法则



We have an abundance of conviction,

我们有着充足的信心，



There is not what you call one shadow of a doubt in our minds that these things work.

用你们的话说，就是：我们没有丝毫的怀疑



We know they work, because they work for us.

这些法则绝对有效，因为它们对我们有效



They can work for you.

对你们，也同样有效



It requires the same degree of conviction that you know they work

只是，你需要做到，像我们一样坚信



And it is more easily had, this conviction is more easily experienced

而要更轻易地拥有这种坚信，更容易地体验到这种坚信



When you stop linking things together in continuity

你必须停止把前前后后发生事情，串联在一起



that don't need to be linked as a means to an end

因为这些事情，无需串联（你只是按照你的想法来串联）



When you truly understand the power of remaining in the present

当你真正明白，活在当下能带给你的强大能量



And defining everything that's happening as the only thing that needs to be going on right now

并且很清楚，现在发生的每件事，都是必须发生的



And it's leading you to nothing else,

而且都是你想要发生的（都是你的最高利益）



You will feel the power of that conviction

你就会在内心，感受到那种坚信



You will know and truly understand what were talking about

你就会明白，就会真正的清楚我们所说的



Make sense?

明白吗？
