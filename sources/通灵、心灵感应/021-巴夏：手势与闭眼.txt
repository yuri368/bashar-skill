问：
I was wondering why you hold Darryl's hands in the triangle while he's channeling you

巴夏：
Very often it is for the aid and assistance in balancing the energy of the body to be capable of allowing the flow through to happen effortlessly.
很多时候，是为了协助平衡他身体的能量，从而使（高频）能量，能够顺畅地流通

问：
OK! And may I ask why you have his eyes closed?
好的！我能问一下，你为什么把他的眼睛给闭上呢？

巴夏：
So that there is not in your terms interruption in the idea of inviting the brain to think about what it is seeing or processing
用你们的话来说，这样的话，他的大脑，就不会因为眼睛所看到的（外在）东西，而产生思考与处理，从而导致通灵的中断

So that it can simply see internally what the patterns are we are receiving and allow for easier flow through.
如此，他（内在视力）就只需要看见我们所感知到的（能量模式）即可，从而能更容易地将其流通

In the sense, it is shutting down some of the senses, so as to not allow the brain to think and interrupt the flow of the channeling
某种意义上讲，这是通过关闭某些（视觉）感官，让大脑不因思考而打断通灵（能量）的流动
