如果你觉得没有百分之百做真实的自己没关系，那就没关系
问：在你成功的做自己之前你真的必须百分之百做自己吗？
巴夏：…这里有一个悖论：当你对没有百分之百做真实的自己感到OK时，你就在百分之百的做真实的自己-因为你知道自己正在经历一个过程，知道自己并不总是处于最佳状态也没关系，这没关系！当你开始对自己进行评判和内疚时，你就会走向另一个方向。
Q; Do you really have to be yourself
100% of the time before you are really
successful at being yourself?
B: ... Here is the paradoxical irony: When you are OK with not being your true self 100% of the time, you are being your true self 100% of the time - because you know you are going through a process and it's
OK to go through a process, and it's OK to know that
you are not always
at your best, and
THAT'S OK!
It's when you
start heaping
judgement and
guilt upon
yourself that you
are going in the
other direction.
