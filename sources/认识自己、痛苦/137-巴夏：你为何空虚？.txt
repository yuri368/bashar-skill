巴夏：你为何空虚？
原创 笑笑^_^学进 巴夏Bashar 2016-06-14


与生活脱节（文字版）

问：

Hello, Bashar!

你好，巴夏！



巴夏：

And to you, good day!

你好！



问：

Hi, I was wondering why I feel so disconnected in life.

嗨，我感觉自己跟生活严重脱节（空虚），我想知道为什么



巴夏：

Well, there can be many different kinds of reasons for that.

这有很多种不同原因



Sometimes the idea of disconnection is again, you're not really being yourself as fully as you could be.

有时候是因为你没有尽自己最大能力、去完整地做你自己



And so you are disconnected,

于是你感觉与生活脱节



or at least having an experience of disconnection from yourself or from source.

或是体验到你与自己、或是与“源头”（神）断开连接



On the other hand, sometimes disconnection is a positive thing,

从另一个角度看，有时候“脱节”也是一件好事



Because in order to reorient yourself from one reality, you have to disorient yourself from another.

因为有时候为了“重定向”你自己，你必须与目前情况的脱离



And sometimes that can be experienced as a temporary disconnection.

还有可能是你只是想短时间地体验与“源头”断开连接的感觉



But what kind of disconnected experience are you actually describing,

那关于你体验到的“断开连接体验”是什么



can you be more specific?

你能否说清楚点？



问：

Yes! In relationship, I don't feel like I emotionally connected to people,

好，在人际关系中，我感觉不到与其他人的情感共鸣



or I don't even know what my feelings are.

我甚至不知道自己的真实感受是什么。



巴夏：

All right. May I ask you a question?

好，我可以问你一个问题吗？



问：

Yes!

可以！



巴夏：

If you did connect to people in those relationships in the way that you say you might like to,

如果在你的人际关系中，如你所愿地，跟他人有“情感共鸣”（连接）



What are you afraid might happen?

你最害怕发生的事情是什么？



问：

Possibly I would be hurt.

可能我会被伤害吧。



巴夏：

How can you be hurt?

你会被怎么伤害呢？



问：

If people reject me?

可能有人会拒绝我



巴夏：

Then that means that you're getting a reflection of something within yourself that you're rejecting.

那意味着你内在在拒绝某些东西，于是在你现实生活中出现这么一个“拒绝的反射”



So if you stop rejecting yourself,

所以，当你你不再拒绝你自己，



you don't need to react anyone else who might choose to reject you.

别人对你的拒绝，就不会激发其你的“反应”（条件反射）



Because you will understand that it has nothing to do with you,

因为你会明白，他人的拒绝跟你没有半毛钱关系



That's their issue, and not yours,

那是他们的问题，与你无关



and you won't take it personally.

于是你也不会把这事放心上



Does that make sense?

明白吗？



So what aspects of your own being are you afraid of so much that you're rejecting them?

能否说说你自己本人的哪种特质让你感觉害怕，以至拒绝它们？



That you won't let them out, that you will express them?

你不敢释放出来，你不敢表达出来？



What parts of your joy are you restricting, and resisting, and limiting in your life?

你在限制，压抑和控制在你的生活中的哪部分“喜悦”呢？



Can you answer that?

你能否回答这个问题？



问：

I don't know!

我不知道！



巴夏：

Sure you do!

你肯定知道！



Let's ask the question in a different way.

我换个方式问你吧。



Are you ready?

你准备好了吗？



问：

Yes!

是的！



巴夏：

Are you to the best of your ability every moment that you can, acting on your highest joy in life?  

你生活中的每一刻，你都尽自己最大努力，出发于你的最高的喜悦，去不断地行动吗？



Yes or no?

回答是还是不是？



Hesitation means no.

犹豫就意味着 “不是”



问：

I just heard that from you recently.

我最近才听到你说这个



巴夏：

All right.

好吧。



Is that something that excites you to do, to act on your highest excitement

你能否在你兴奋喜悦的事情上，



to the best of your ability with no insistence on a particular outcome?

尽你最大能力去不断行动，而不执着与某种特定的结果



Do you believe you can do that?

你相信你做得到吗？



问：

Yes!

我相信！



巴夏：

All right, then the more you start doing that,

那你越是不断地这样去行动



the more you will discover anything that holds you back from being yourself.

你将发现越来越多那些限制你“成为自己”的一切东西（信念系统）



Because when you really choose, when you really choose to be yourself,

因为当你真正做出选择，当你真正选择“做自己”



and that's the only choice that makes sense to you,

而且那是你唯一想做的选择



then you will not choose the things that prevent you from being that.

那你自然而然不会选择那些限制自己选项



And you will discover what they are, as you are acting on your highest excitement,

当你你在出发于自己的兴奋，持续不断的行动过程中，



they will reveal themselves to you,

你会发现一个个限制性信念系统，



and one by one, you will let go of the things that are not the you you prefer to be.

你将不再听信它们，并且你将逐一释放它们



That's how it works.

这就是做你兴奋的事的运作方式



Are you willing to go through that process?

你愿意完整地体验这个过程吗？



问：

Yes!

愿意！



巴夏：

Are you willing to take responsibility for the things that you don't prefer,

你愿意对你生活中不喜欢的事情负担起你的责任，



as well as the things that you do in your life?

如同你对待自己喜欢的事情那样吗？



问：

Yes!

是的！



Because you're the one creating your experience,

因为你所有的一切体验都是你自己创造的



So you have to take responsibility for your part of it, and what you experience totally!

所以你要负担其你的责任，你的一切体验的责任



问：

Yes!

好！



巴夏：

Are right.

好！



And when you take responsibility for it.

当你能负担起你的责任



You will then be exhibiting the ability to respond,

这说明有能力对事件做出反应（展示出对事件做出反应的能力）



Which means you are self empowered.

就意味着你赋予自己能力



Which means you will keep moving forward,

意味着你将不断前进



Which means you will keep expanding,

意味着你将不断扩展



Which means you will keep growing,

意味着你将不断成长



Which means you will keep having more and more fun,

意味着你将拥有越来越多的乐趣



Which means you will learn how to handle things in a more accomplished holistic way.

也意味着你将学会以全局视角的方式来处理事情



Does that soud like fun?

这听起来是否其乐无穷？



问：

Yes!

是的！



巴夏：

Then you have to be willing to take the first step,

那么你必须有意愿迈出第一步



and act on your highest excitement in whatever form it may come,

在你最高兴奋的事件上付诸行动，不论是何种方式的兴奋



Even if it seems to contain just a little more excitement than any other option,

即使这个选项只比另外一个选项多一点“兴奋”



That's your higher mind telling you that's the thing to do right now.

多的一点的兴奋，就是你的高级智慧在告诉你：现在就做这个！



Follow the thread of the excitement, not the way things look on the surface.

跟着“兴奋”的指引，而非只看事情的表面



You understand? Does this help you? Are you sure?

你明白吗？这对你有帮助吗？你肯定？



问：

YES!!!

可以！！



巴夏：

All right!

很好！
