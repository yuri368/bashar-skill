巴夏:父母如何帮助孩子学习
原创 笑笑^_^学进 巴夏Bashar 2016-09-07



问：

Do you have any idea about the best way to be a parent of young child.

你能否给做父母的一些好建议，



in a way of you don’t get in the way of their excitement.

好让我们能更好地帮助孩子去追求他们的喜悦、兴奋，而非成为其拦路虎？



巴夏：

Allow yourself to be clever and creative enough,

首先让自己变得更聪明、更有创造力，



to explore with the child what their greatest excitement is.

用心与孩子相处，与他们一起探索他们最大的兴奋是什么。



And then any lesson you know it's important for them to learn,

然后在他们该学的重要的课程上



attempt to use your creativity to adapt the lesson to their excitement,

发挥你的创造力，让这个课程跟他们的兴奋（激情）相结合



rather than adapting their excitement to the lesson.

而非试图让他们的兴奋转向课程上。



Let them learn what they need to learn in the way that excites them.

让他们在兴奋的状态下学会他们该学的。



So you, in that sense, must be multifaceted enough and imaginative enough

所以，你必须多才多艺、富有想象，



to explore with them how to adapt the lesson to their excitement.

用心跟他们一起探索，让课程跟他们的兴奋相结合。



So that you can engage their excitement, allows them to want to learn that thing.

这样你就可以激发他们，让他们激情澎湃地想要学习这些课程。



by exploring with them, how they could go about learning it in the way that's most exciting for them. 不断地与他们一起探索，让他们可以兴致勃勃地学习这些课程。



And interactivity is also a key.

互动也是关键（也是窍门）。



The more realistic,

学的东西得贴近生活



the more you invite them to participate in scenarios that are highly interactive,

努力创造场景让他们将所学的与他人交互



where they have to problem solving realistic scenarios,

让他们在场景中去解决实际问题。



then the more they will be capable of applying their own excitement to the task.

那么他们将不断激发自己的“兴奋”去完成任务。



You follow? Does that help?

明白吗？这对你有帮助吗？



Eventually all your school systems will do this.

最终你们所有的教学体制都将是这样的。



问：

I hope so. Thank you!

我希望如此。谢谢你！



巴夏：

You don't have to hope so. 

你不必希望如此。



The idea is that it’s your planet.

这个是你们的星球。



If that's something that excites you, then invent the systems.

如果这让你兴奋，那么就去开发这样的系统。



问：

All right, thank you.

好的，谢谢。
