巴夏：教给孩子的三门课
原创 笑笑^_^学进 巴夏Bashar 2016-04-11



问：

Can you tell me more about how to help the children and people I'm working with. 

你能否说说我该怎么做，才能更好地帮助一起共事的女人和孩子



巴夏：

How to help the children on the earth? 

如何帮助地球上的孩子吗？



问：

Technically, yes! 

可以这么说



巴夏：

Help them what? 

帮他们什么呢？



问：

In general! 

总体上的



巴夏：

All right! 

好！



In general, 

总体上来讲



You can allow them in their education a few specific things. 

有些东西，你们可以教给孩子



Number one. 

第一



Find out what excites them, 

找出他们所兴奋东西



And adapt the lessons they need to learn to their excitement, 

对他们必须学的课程进行改编，来迎合他们的兴奋



Not the other way around. 

而不是反过来（让学生适应课程）



Number two. 

第二



Teach them in a safe environment 

在安全的环境中



How to make choices 

教他们如何做决定



And what the consequences of their choices and actions actually are. 

以及他们的选择与行动 所带来的相应的后果



Number three 

第三



Teach them that they are totally self-empowered 

让他们明白 他们每个人都非常强大的！



They are as powerful as they need to be 

他们有足够的能力



To have anything they need in life 

去获得任何他们想要的东西



Without having to hurt himself or anyone else in order to get it 

而不需要通过伤害自己或者他人



Just teach those three lessons 

只要教会孩子们这三门课



And you will have a powerful next generation of children 

那你们的下一代  将会非常强大！



And of course, remember 

当然，还要记住



That the generations of children being born on your planet now 

现在出生在地球上的儿童



are not just new generations. 

不仅仅只是新一代儿童



They are actually a new species 

他们其实是新物种（新新人类）



And they are not forgetting as much of who they are. 

他们并没有像你们一样 忘记“我是谁”



So one of the things you can teach the children 

所以，其中一个你们可以教他们的



Is how 

是如何



To teach you

教你们



who you are 

你是谁



By listening

通过听他们



to the fact that many of them know more than the adults who they are 

看他们对“我是谁”，比成年人都懂得多得多  这个事实



问：

I understand that also. 

这个我也明白



巴夏：

All right. 

好的！
